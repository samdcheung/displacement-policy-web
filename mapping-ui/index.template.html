The six desk-review questions

How the evidence is handled

The Mapping draws on published laws, government sources, UN and development-bank documents, peer-reviewed research and evaluations. Individual claims should be linked to identifiable passages and should not exceed what those sources establish.

  • Formal design ≠ implementation: a written policy does not establish actual inclusion.
  • Funding announced ≠ money spent: sources, allocations and expenditure are distinct.
  • Absence of documentation ≠ failure: unknowns remain explicit.
  • Inclusion is conditional: ordinary systems may be unsafe or exclusionary for particular populations.
  • No country scores: compare mechanisms and consequences, not national grades.
